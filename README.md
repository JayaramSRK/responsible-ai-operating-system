# Responsible AI Operating System

Practical operating patterns for adopting generative AI with clear accountability, proportionate controls, and measurable business outcomes.

## Why this exists

AI adoption fails when policy, product delivery, security, and executive decision-making operate as separate systems. This repository provides a lightweight reference model that connects them.

## Operating model

1. **Classify the use case** — purpose, users, data sensitivity, impact, and autonomy.
2. **Assign accountable ownership** — business owner, product owner, risk owner, and technical owner.
3. **Apply proportionate controls** — privacy, security, evaluation, human oversight, monitoring, and incident response.
4. **Measure outcomes** — adoption quality, risk signals, user value, cost, and realized benefits.
5. **Improve continuously** — review evidence, update controls, and retire low-value use cases.

## Included

- AI use-case intake and triage
- Governance roles and decision rights
- Control catalog starter
- Evaluation and monitoring prompts
- Executive scorecard design

## Status

Early-stage reference implementation. Contributions are welcome from practitioners building safe, useful, and accountable AI systems.
